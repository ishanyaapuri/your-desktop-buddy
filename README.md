# 🐾 Your Desktop Buddy

A lightweight, animated companion for Windows that lives right on your screen to keep you company, help you study, and remind you to take care of yourself.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Use Any Character:** Comes with a default hand-drawn character, or upload your own transparent `.png` right inside the Settings menu!
- 🛋️ **Chill Mode:** Gentle reminders for posture, relaxing, eye relief, and cozy companionship.
- 🎯 **Study / Focus Mode:** Anti-distraction, anti-procrastination, and study encouragement.
- 🫁 **Guided Breathing Bubble:** Interactive 12-second visual breathwork balloon (inhale, hold, exhale).
- 💧 **Hydration Reminders:** Automatic water checks every 30 minutes.
- ⏰ **Real-Time Clock Sync:** Relevant greetings based on real-world morning, afternoon, evening, and late night.
- 📅 **Days Together Counter:** Celebrates each unique day you spend working together.
- ⚙️ **Settings Dashboard:** Easily adjust visit frequencies, pet size, quiet hours, and auto-startup.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (For Non-Tech Users)

### 1. Prerequisite
Make sure you have **[Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)** (LTS version) installed on your computer.

### 2. Download & Run
1. Click the green **`<> Code`** button above → **Download ZIP**.
2. Extract the ZIP folder anywhere on your PC.
3. Double-click **`Setup-And-Start.bat`**. *(Installs everything and launches your buddy automatically!)*
4. For all future launches, just double-click **`Start-Desktop-Buddy.bat`**.

---

## 🎮 Controls

Your buddy lives quietly down by your clock in the **Windows Taskbar Tray**:

- **Left-Click or Right-Click Tray Icon:** Opens the control menu:
  - ✨ **Visit Me Now:** Summons your buddy immediately.
  - 🎯 **Study / Focus Mode:** 1-click toggle between Study and Chill modes.
  - ⚙️ **Settings:** Change intervals, pet size, or upload a custom character image.
  - 🟢 **Turn ON / 🔴 Turn OFF:** Master sleep switch.
  - ⏱️ **Snooze:** Pause visits for 30m or 1 hour.
  - 📍 **Reset Position:** Snaps your buddy back to the bottom-right corner.
  - ❌ **Quit Desktop Buddy:** Closes the app.
- **Move Buddy:** Click and drag your buddy anywhere on your screen. He permanently remembers where you place him!
